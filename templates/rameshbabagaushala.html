About Ramesh Baba Gaushala

Ramesh Baba Gaushala is a sacred cow sanctuary founded by the revered Ramesh Baba. It is a place dedicated to the protection, care, and service of cows, considered sacred in Hindu tradition.

Under the loving care of Ramesh Baba and his devoted team, this gaushala has become a beacon of compassion and spiritual service in Barsana. It embodies the philosophy of "Gau Seva" (cow service) as a path to spiritual enlightenment.

Devotees visiting this gaushala participate in the sacred service of feeding and caring for cows, experiencing the joy of serving these divine creatures in the holy land of Braj.
