About Sankri Khor

Sankri Khor is a unique and mystical location in Barsana, known for its deep gorge and spiritual significance. The name refers to a narrow passage between high rocky cliffs.

According to local traditions, this sacred spot holds immense spiritual power. Many pilgrims believe that visiting Sankri Khor and meditating in this serene location brings clarity and inner peace.

The natural landscape and the mystical atmosphere of Sankri Khor make it a favorite destination for devotees seeking a deeper spiritual experience in Braj.
