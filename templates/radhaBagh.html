About Radha Bagh

Radha Bagh is a beautiful and serene garden in Barsana dedicated to Radha Rani, the supreme source of all love and devotion in the divine realm.

This sacred garden is believed to be the place where Radha spent her divine moments in prayer and meditation. The peaceful surroundings and blooming flowers create an atmosphere of spiritual tranquility.

Devotees visit Radha Bagh to experience the gentle presence of Radha Rani and to meditate on her infinite love and grace.
