The Beloved Pond of Radha

About Priyakund

Priyakund is a sacred pond in Barsana with immense spiritual significance. According to legend, this is where Radha Rani and her sakhis took their divine bath.

The name "Priya" means beloved, and this kund (pond) is believed to be the beloved bathing place of Radha Rani herself. The waters of this pond are considered sacred and purifying.

Devotees visit Priyakund to experience the divine presence of Radha Rani and to touch the sacred waters that have been blessed by her divine presence.
