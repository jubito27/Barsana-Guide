Maan Garh (Maan Mandir)

The Divine Peak of Radha Rani's Loving Anger

About Maan Garh Mandir

Maan Garh, popular as Maan Mandir, is situated on top of one of the four peaks of Bhanugarh hill in Barsana. This sacred site is historically dedicated to the sweet pastime where Shri Radha Rani entered into 'Maan' (a state of loving, transcendental pique or anger) towards Shri Krishna.

According to regional lore, Shri Krishna took the form of a dynamic peacock, a dynamic lover, and even washed Shri Radha's feet here just to placate her loving anger. The temple sits amidst breathtaking scenic beauty, managed closely under the spiritual guidance of local saints like Ramesh Baba Ji, promoting environment and heritage protection.

Right below the peak lies the divine Gehra Kund and the narrow pass of Sankari Khor, making it a critical halt for pilgrims executing the heritage Braj Chaurasi Kos Yatra.
