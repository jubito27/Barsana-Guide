Vilas Garh (Vilas Mandir)

The Enchanting Peak of Radha Krishna's Divine Playfulness

About Vilas Garh Mandir

Vilas Garh, also known as Vilas Mandir, rests beautifully on the third peak of the sacred Bhanugarh hill in Barsana. This mystical place is dedicated to the sweet, transcendental, and playful pastimes (Vilas Lila) of Priya-Priyatam, where they shared moments of laughter, hide-and-seek, and divine association.

The spiritual vibration here highlights the peak of Madhurya Rasa, where the divine couple and their close sakhis would gather away from worldly eyes. The surroundings are filled with natural serenity, vibrant trees, and a peaceful environment that automatically draws mind towards devotion.

A short walk from the main path connects you directly to this tranquil temple. It is a vital spot for devotees performing the Braj Parikrama who wish to meditate upon the joyful, intimate, and loving side of Shri Radha Rani's daily pastimes.
