Mor Kuti (Mor Kutir)

The Divine Peak of Radha Krishna's Celestial Peacock Dance

About Mor Kuti Mandir

Mor Kuti (also known as Mor Kutir) is situated on the fourth sacred peak of Bhanugarh hill in Barsana. This legendary site holds a very sweet place in Braj history, commemorating the Mayur Lila where Shri Radha Rani and Shri Krishna danced in the ecstasy of divine love.

According to the pastimes, once Shri Radha entered a transcendental mood where she desired to see a peacock dance. To fulfill her wish instantly, Rasraj Shri Krishna himself transformed into a magnificent, charming peacock and performed an enchanting dance that left everyone mesmerized.

The temple features beautiful paintings and reliefs depicting this divine dance. The valley surrounding Mor Kuti is still naturally inhabited by dozens of peacocks, keeping the ancient spiritual aura alive for modern pilgrims executing the Chaurasi Kos Yatra.
