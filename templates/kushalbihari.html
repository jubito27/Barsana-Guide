About Kushal Bihari Ji Mandir

Kushal Bihari Ji Mandir is a cherished shrine in Barsana, dedicated to Lord Krishna as the gentle guardian of devotees' happiness and well-being.

This peaceful temple is known for its beautiful idol, sacred rituals, and welcoming atmosphere where pilgrims gather to offer prayers and seek blessings.

The temple is especially visited during Holi and Janmashtami, when the atmosphere becomes vibrant with devotional songs, colorful traditions, and fragrant flower offerings.
