Dan Garh (Dan Bihari Mandir)

The Sacred Peak of Shri Krishna's Divine Tax Pastimes

About Dan Garh Mandir

Dan Garh, home to the beautiful Dan Bihari Mandir, is another magnificent peak situated on the hills of Barsana. This temple commemorates the enchanting Dan Lila, where Kanha and his cowherd friends would playfuly stop Radha Rani and the Gopis to demand a "tax" of milk, yogurt, and butter.

The core spiritual essence of this place teaches total surrender in love, as the Gopis gladly offered their milk products to Shri Krishna. The temple holds ancient idols and exudes an intense atmosphere of Vatsalya and Madhurya Bhava.

Pilgrims visiting Dan Garh are greeted with a panoramic view of the entire Braj landscape. Its close connection to Sankari Khor (the narrow valley where Krishna intercepted the Gopis) makes it a deeply revered site during the annual Lathmar Holi and Chaurasi Kos Parikrama.
