About 108 Kutiya

108 Kutiya is a unique spiritual complex in Barsana consisting of 108 small cottages, each dedicated to one of the 108 names of Lord Krishna.

The number 108 holds great significance in Hindu spirituality, representing the divine and cosmic completeness. These 108 cottages create a sacred circle of devotion.

Devotees often visit each kutiya to meditate on the different names and aspects of Krishna, experiencing a comprehensive spiritual journey through his divine manifestations.
